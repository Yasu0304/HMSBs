AI-Assisted Upper Airway Ultrasound for OSA Risk Assessment

Beyond Sleep Data: Bringing Upper Airway Anatomy into Clinical Assessment.

Obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) is a common sleep-related breathing disorder that requires careful clinical evaluation. While traditional sleep studies provide essential physiological information, understanding upper airway anatomy and surrounding soft tissue characteristics can offer additional insights for patient assessment.

HMS offers an AI-assisted upper airway ultrasound solution that combines non-invasive imaging, standardized scanning, and quantitative analysis to help healthcare professionals evaluate upper airway structures in approximately 10 minutes.

By adding anatomical imaging to existing sleep medicine workflows, this solution supports OSA risk assessment, clinical evaluation, and follow-up planning—helping healthcare providers make more informed decisions based on patient-specific information.

Why Upper Airway Ultrasound?

Adding Anatomical Insights to Sleep Apnea Evaluation

Conventional sleep studies evaluate physiological events during sleep, while upper airway ultrasound provides a complementary view of airway anatomy.
Using AI-assisted image analysis, healthcare professionals can examine airway dimensions, tongue morphology, and surrounding soft tissue characteristics to gain additional information relevant to OSA risk assessment.

Supporting More Efficient Clinical Assessment

The examination can be performed while the patient is awake, without anesthesia or exposure to ionizing radiation.
With an assessment time of approximately 10 minutes, upper airway ultrasound offers a practical approach to collecting anatomical information in outpatient clinical settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q1｜ What can the system measure?
    The system provides quantitative measurements of airspace depth, airspace width, tissue thickness, and tissue width. Region-of-interest analysis enables targeted evaluation of selected airway and soft tissue structures.

  • Q2｜ Does the system include an ultrasound scanner?
    The system uses upper airway images acquired from compatible ultrasound equipment. Available hardware packages and ultrasound scanner compatibility should be confirmed with HMS before purchase.

  • Q3｜ What is the purpose of the Laser Alignment Tool?
    The Laser Alignment Tool helps standardize patient head and ultrasound probe positioning, supporting more consistent image acquisition and improved comparability between examinations.

  • Q4｜ How long does an examination take?
    Upper airway ultrasound assessment takes approximately 10 minutes under the described scanning workflow. Actual examination time may vary based on patient and clinical requirements.

  • Q5｜ Can the system diagnose obstructive sleep apnea?
    The system provides upper airway imaging and quantitative analysis to assist clinical evaluation. It is not a standalone OSA diagnostic test and does not replace clinically indicated PSG, HSAT, or other diagnostic procedures.
