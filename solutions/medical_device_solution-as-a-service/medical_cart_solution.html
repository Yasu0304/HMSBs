Medical Cart Solutions

Healthcare mobility solutions for hospitals, clinics, long-term care facilities, and medical device partners in the U.S. market.

HMS provides medical cart solutions designed to support the daily needs of healthcare environments, from mobile nursing and bedside care to telehealth, point-of-care workflows, and medical device mobility.

As a U.S.-focused healthcare channel and solution promotion partner, HMS helps hospitals, clinics, long-term care facilities, system integrators, and medical device companies select and configure the right medical carts, medical-grade computers, displays, power modules, mounting accessories, and healthcare peripherals for real clinical use.

Medical carts are no longer just equipment carriers. In modern healthcare settings, they have become mobile workstations that support clinical documentation, patient communication, remote consultation, device deployment, and care team efficiency. With the right configuration, a medical cart can become a mobile nursing station, telemedicine workstation, point-of-care computer cart, or medical device mobility platform.

HMS focuses on practical deployment, product fit, and solution readiness. We help customers evaluate suitable configurations, simplify product selection, and connect medical cart solutions with real healthcare workflows.

Application-Based Configuration

Mobile Nursing and Bedside Care

For ward rounds, bedside care, nursing documentation, and patient data access, HMS provides mobile nursing cart configurations that can integrate medical-grade computers, touch displays, keyboard trays, barcode scanner holders, drawers, storage modules, and power systems.
These solutions help nursing teams access patient information, update medical records, verify orders, and complete care tasks more efficiently between nursing stations, patient rooms, and bedside environments.

Ward rounds Bedside care Nursing documentation EMR / EHR access Medication and care coordination support
Telehealth and Virtual Rounding

For telehealth, virtual rounding, remote consultation, long-term care, and cross-site medical collaboration, HMS supports telehealth cart configurations that can integrate cameras, displays, tablets, medical-grade computers, audio devices, network equipment, and power modules.
These mobile telehealth workstations help healthcare teams extend care access, support remote communication, and deploy telemedicine services more efficiently in hospitals, clinics, senior care communities, and long-term care facilities.

Telehealth visits Virtual rounding Remote consultation Long-term care facility support Specialist collaboration Cross-location healthcare communication
Point-of-Care Medical Computer Carts

For clinical information systems, bedside data access, and point-of-care workflows, HMS provides medical computer cart configurations that can support medical-grade computers, touch panel PCs, displays, keyboards, mice, barcode scanners, and other peripherals.
These carts help healthcare teams bring information access closer to the point of care, improving workflow efficiency and supporting more flexible clinical operations.

EMR / EHR access Bedside data access Clinical documentation Mobile medical computing Nursing station and ward workflows Point-of-care workstation deployment
Tablet and Laptop Carts

For lightweight healthcare applications, HMS offers tablet and laptop cart configurations for outpatient areas, patient education, registration, long-term care, and mobile care documentation.
These carts can be configured with tablet holders, laptop trays, charging modules, anti-theft locks, cable management, and compact accessories, making them suitable for space-limited or high-mobility environments.

Patient education Mobile registration Outpatient support Lightweight care documentation Mobile administrative workflows
