Medical Mask Supply & Distribution Solutions

Reliable medical mask supply and distribution support for U.S. hospitals, healthcare facilities, and end users.

HMS provides reliable medical mask supply and distribution solutions for U.S. hospitals, clinics, surgery centers, long-term care facilities, healthcare procurement teams, and end users. As a U.S. distributor partner and global medical device distributor, HMS offers a structured portfolio of medical mask options, including Level 1 Medical Masks, Level 2 Medical Masks, Tie-on Surgical Masks, and Advanced Protection Medical Masks. By combining product portfolio knowledge with medical device distribution support, HMS helps customers identify suitable mask options for routine clinical use, patient care, surgical environments, and high-protection applications while simplifying product evaluation, sourcing, and procurement for essential medical PPE.

Mask Options by Healthcare Use

General Clinical Use and Outpatient Care

General Clinical Use and Outpatient Care

For outpatient clinics, routine consultations, and general healthcare support areas, HMS provides Level 1 Medical Mask options designed for basic protection needs. These masks emphasize lightweight comfort, breathability, and daily wearability for routine clinical environments.

Ward Care and Daily Patient Care

Ward Care and Daily Patient Care

For hospital wards, nursing stations, examination rooms, and daily patient care settings, HMS offers Level 2 Medical Mask options that support higher droplet protection needs while maintaining comfort for frequent clinical use.

Surgical and Procedural Environments

Surgical and Procedural Environments

For operating rooms, surgery centers, treatment areas, and procedural workflows, HMS provides Tie-on Surgical Mask options with secure fastening and stable wear. These products support longer-duration use and reliable positioning in professional healthcare environments.

High-Protection Healthcare Settings

High-Protection Healthcare Settings

For high-risk healthcare environments or settings with elevated respiratory protection requirements, HMS supports Advanced Protection Medical Mask options designed with attention to fit, filtration performance, and practical protection needs.
