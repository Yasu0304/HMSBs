ACCEL-A2203

22” Medical AI All-in-One PC with 14th Gen Core I CPU and MXM Graphics

Feature

  • NVIDIA RTX A1000/A2000/A4500 MXM Graphics
  • Intel® 14th generation Core™ i9/ i7/ i5 / i3 Processor
  • Support Dual Channel DDR5 5600Mhz SODIMM up to 96GB
  • 22" Full HD High Contrast LCD w/ Capacitive Multi-Touch Screen
  • 2.5 Gigabit LAN x 1, Gigabit LAN x 1
  • High Speed USB 3.2 Gen2 Ports
  • 8MP Camera with Mic (optional)
  • Imprivata RFID Reader (optional)
Download Contact Us

ACCEL-A2203 is a 22” all-in-one medical AI panel PC combining clinical-grade touchscreen functionality with embedded AI edge computing. It is built to power real-time inference, imaging, and diagnostics directly at the point of care.

  • 21.5” Full HD touchscreen with medical-grade housing (IP65 front)
  • Integrated NVIDIA® Jetson AGX Orin™ for AI workloads
  • Intel® Tiger Lake-UP3 i7/i5/i3 processor for system-level performance
  • EN 60601-1 certified for clinical safety
  • Fanless, hygienic design ideal for OR, ICU, and nurse stations
  • Rich I/O: USB 3.2, HDMI, LAN, COM, GPIO
  • Optional: camera, RFID, smart card reader, barcode scanner
  • Wall-mount and cart-compatible for flexible deployment
  • AI Diagnostic Stations: Embedded inference engine for imaging or pathology screening
  • Smart Nursing Stations: Data visualization, vitals analysis, and patient insights
  • OR Workstations: Real-time surgical imaging enhancement and overlay
  • Clinical Monitoring: Local AI processing for anomaly detection and alerting
  • Medical Cart Integration: Combines display, computing, and AI in one compact unit
  • Telemedicine Hubs: Supports HD video, edge analytics, and remote diagnostics
