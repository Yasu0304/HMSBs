Medical Cart Series

From mobile nursing and telemedicine to medical device mobility, HMS provides medical cart and medical computer integration solutions designed for different clinical applications and market needs.

Feature

  • Provides medical cart solutions for mobile nursing, telehealth, point-of-care workflows, long-term care, and medical device mobility
  • Supports integration with medical-grade computers, touch panel PCs, tablets, laptops, displays, cameras, barcode scanners, and healthcare peripherals
  • Flexible configuration options with mounting accessories, power modules, storage modules, cable management, and medical-grade casters
  • Helps hospitals, clinics, long-term care facilities, system integrators, distributors, and medical device partners evaluate suitable cart solutions
  • Designed to support EMR / EHR access, bedside care, virtual rounding, clinical documentation, patient education, and mobile equipment deployment
  • Balances mobility, stability, ergonomics, device compatibility, workflow efficiency, and practical healthcare deployment
  • Supports customized configurations based on device size, weight, mounting requirements, power needs, and application environment

HMS offers a flexible Medical Cart Series for hospitals, clinics, long-term care facilities, telehealth programs, and medical device projects. As a U.S.-focused healthcare solution partner, HMS helps customers configure medical carts with computers, displays, cameras, power modules, mounting accessories, and healthcare peripherals to support mobile nursing, telehealth, point-of-care workflows, and medical device mobility.

Mobile Nursing and Bedside Care

Designed for ward rounds, bedside care, nursing documentation, medication workflow support, and EMR / EHR access. The mobile nursing cart can be configured with medical-grade computers, touch displays, keyboard trays, barcode scanner holders, drawers, storage modules, and power systems to help nursing teams improve mobility and workflow efficiency.

Telehealth and Virtual Rounding

Designed for telemedicine, virtual rounding, remote consultation, long-term care facility support, and cross-location healthcare communication. The telehealth cart can integrate cameras, displays, tablets, medical-grade computers, audio devices, network equipment, and power modules to create a mobile telehealth workstation.

Point-of-Care Medical Computing

Designed for clinical information systems, bedside data access, and point-of-care workflows. The medical computer cart can be equipped with medical-grade computers, touch panel PCs, displays, keyboards, mice, barcode scanners, and other peripherals to bring information access closer to the patient.

Tablet and Laptop Applications

Designed for lightweight healthcare information applications, outpatient areas, patient education, mobile registration, long-term care management, and mobile administrative workflows. The tablet and laptop cart can be configured with tablet holders, laptop trays, anti-theft locks, charging modules, compact storage, and cable management accessories.
