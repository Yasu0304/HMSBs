Transforming Upper Airway Ultrasound into Quantitative Anatomical Insights
The AI-Powered Upper Airway Ultrasound Assessment System combines ultrasound image acquisition, AI-assisted image analysis, and standardized positioning technology to provide detailed visualization and quantitative evaluation of upper airway structures.
Through a dedicated software interface, healthcare professionals can examine airway dimensions, tongue morphology, and surrounding soft tissue characteristics.
The system provides quantitative measurements of airway depth, width, and tissue dimensions, helping clinicians gain additional anatomical information for upper airway assessment and obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) risk evaluation.

Multi-Region Analysis for Detailed Airway Measurements
The system supports imaging and analysis of multiple anatomical regions, including the velum, oropharynx, tongue base, and epiglottis.
With AI-assisted image analysis and region-of-interest (ROI) selection, clinicians can review specific airway and tissue regions and obtain quantitative measurements.

  • Airspace Depth:
    Measures the depth of the upper airway space.
  • Airspace Width:
    Provides quantitative measurements of airway width.
  • Tissue Thickness:
    Measures the thickness of the tongue and surrounding soft tissues.
  • Tissue Width:
    Evaluates soft tissue width and related anatomical dimensions.
  • Region-of-Interest (ROI) Analysis:
    Supports targeted image analysis and measurement of selected airway and tissue regions.

    • Laser-Assisted Positioning for Consistent Image Acquisition
    The system incorporates a Laser Alignment Tool (LAT) featuring three laser alignment beams and an adjustable positioning mechanism.
    This positioning technology helps operators align the patient's head and ultrasound probe during scanning.
    By supporting standardized scanning positions, the system helps reduce positioning-related image variations and improve the comparability of quantitative measurements across examinations.

    Dedicated Analysis Interface & Reporting
    The system provides a specialized image analysis interface that displays upper airway ultrasound images, regions of interest, quantitative measurements, and related statistical information.
    Application-specific reports consolidate imaging findings and measurement data, allowing healthcare professionals to review upper airway anatomical characteristics and support further clinical evaluation.

    System Components

    01｜ Ultrasound Image Acquisition

    Upper airway images are acquired using compatible ultrasound equipment and serve as the source data for subsequent image analysis.

    02｜ Laser Alignment Tool

    A laser-assisted positioning device with three alignment beams and an adjustable mechanism helps standardize patient head and ultrasound probe positioning.

    03｜ AI Image Analysis Software

    A computer-based software interface supports upper airway image visualization, ROI selection, anatomical measurements, and quantitative analysis.

    04｜ Quantitative Assessment Reports

    Dedicated reports consolidate upper airway images, measurement results, and related anatomical assessment information for clinical review.

    Available hardware, compatible ultrasound equipment, and supplied system components are subject to configuration confirmation.
