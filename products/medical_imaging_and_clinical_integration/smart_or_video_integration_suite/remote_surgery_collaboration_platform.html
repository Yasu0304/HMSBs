Remote Surgery Collaboration Platform

Bring the operating room to every screen, in real time.

Feature

  • Surgery-grade remote streaming
    Provides telemedicine and surgical streaming services designed for smart operating rooms, with medical-grade safety in mind.
  • Anytime, anywhere access
    Enables physicians to join Live surgery sessions from phones, tablets, or computers without being constrained by OR space.
  • One-click Live surgery
    Starts Live Surgery with a single action, eliminating the need for traditional broadcast vendors, SNG vehicles, and additional A/V setups.
  • Multi-angle real-time video
    Offers multi-angle surgical images with quick switching between device views so remote participants can follow every detail.
  • Interactive whiteboard
    Includes an interactive whiteboard for real-time annotation, explanation, and discussion during remote sessions.
  • One-to-one and one-to-many sessions
    Supports point-to-point guidance and multi-site Live transmission across time zones, with smooth, non-delayed reception.

The Remote Surgery Collaboration Platform connects operating rooms, experts, and trainees through secure, high-quality surgical streaming. With one-click Live surgery, hospitals can easily host remote proctoring, cross-site consultations, and international teaching sessions—without complex broadcast setups. Multi-angle real-time video and an interactive whiteboard maximize communication efficiency, helping experienced surgeons guide remote teams and enabling students to learn from live cases even when OR access is limited.

  • Remote surgical guidance and proctoring
  • Live surgery broadcasts for academic seminars and conferences
  • Cross-hospital or cross-border case discussions
  • Distance learning for residents and fellows, especially when OR access is restricted
Client devices Supports participation from smartphones, tablets, and PCs.
Session types One-to-one and one-to-many Live surgery sessions; multi-site participation across time zones.
Video capabilities Multi-angle real-time surgical video with view switching between different medical device sources.
Collaboration tools Interactive whiteboard for marking and highlighting during live cases.
