HMS at HIMSS US 2026: Building the Intelligent Hospital—From Edge to Cloud

A unified theme connecting bedside devices, AI workflows, and secure infrastructure to help clinicians work faster and smarter.

HMS will bring a hospital-wide, AI-ready blueprint to HIMSS US 2026, showcasing how providers can move from point solutions to an integrated, outcome-driven digital ecosystem. Guided by our promise—First in care, always there—the experience is organized into hands-on zones that reflect real clinical journeys and IT needs.

Exhibition Theme

Empowering Hospitals with Intelligent AI Infrastructure

From device integration and medical imaging to care team collaboration, HMS aligns hardware, software, and managed services so that innovations scale beyond single departments and deliver measurable gains in workflow, safety, and cost.

Experience Zones

01 Portable Imaging

Demonstrates portable radiography/fluoro use cases for ER, ICU, and bedside. Focus: faster decision-making with low-dose acquisition, simplified positioning, and secure data flows into PACS/VNA and the EMR.

02 Respiratory Monitoring

Continuous and spot-check monitoring connected to clinician dashboards. Focus: early warnings, streamlined rounding, and escalation workflows with role-based alerts.

03 Surgical Imaging Integration & Management

An OR command view that unifies imaging, video, and device data. Focus: interoperability, latency control, and simplified recording/archive policies for governance and training.

04 Use-Case Spotlight: Fracture & MSK

Walkthrough of imaging + AI triage + reporting that reduces time to consult and improves handoffs.

05 Veterinary Diagnostics

Species-aware chemistry analysis for point-of-care efficiency in animal health networks. Focus: 12-15 minute turnaround and standardized quality controls.

06 Manufacturing & OEM/ODM/EMS

How HMS supports medical device brands with design for compliance, lifecycle supply, and after-sales service. Focus: faster time-to-market and long-horizon reliability.

Why It Matters

Hospitals and health systems need improvements that stick: fewer clicks, clearer signals, and deployable governance. HMS brings the connective tissue—integration expertise, and AI-ready infrastructure—so frontline teams gain speed without sacrificing safety.
